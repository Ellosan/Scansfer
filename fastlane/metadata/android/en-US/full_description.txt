Scansfer moves a photo or video from one Android phone to another using nothing
but a screen and a camera.

One phone turns the file into a stream of QR codes and plays them. The other
phone watches through its camera and rebuilds the file. There is no Wi-Fi, no
Bluetooth, no cable, no account and no server anywhere in the process.

Because the two phones never touch a network, nothing you send can be
intercepted in transit, logged by a provider, or left sitting on someone else's
computer. The file goes from one screen straight into the other camera.

Missed frames are expected and handled. The sender does not number the pieces
and hope they all arrive; it emits an endless stream of coded frames, and the
receiver rebuilds the file once it has collected enough of them. You can look
away, block the camera for a few seconds, or move the phones, and the transfer
carries on rather than failing.

When the last piece arrives the file is checked against a checksum sent with it,
so a transfer either produces a byte-for-byte copy of the original or tells you
it failed. Photos keep their original quality and metadata.

Features:

* Send photos and videos between two phones
* Works completely offline, with no network of any kind
* Requests one permission: the camera
* Three speed settings, with an honest time estimate before you start
* Recovers automatically from missed and unreadable frames
* Verifies the finished file before saving it to your gallery
* No ads, no tracking, no accounts

Be aware that QR codes are a low bandwidth channel. A photo takes a few minutes
and a video takes considerably longer. The app shows you an estimate for your
file before you commit to it.
